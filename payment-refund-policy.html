Payment and Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains the available payment methods, payment verification process, order cancellations, and refund requests for Gmail Services | Digital Solutions.

1. Available Payment Methods

  • GCash: Payment through our officially provided GCash payment details.
  • Bank Transfer: Payment through our officially provided bank payment details.
  • Cash on Delivery / Pay in Person: Payment arrangements must be confirmed with us before the service is provided.

2. Payment Verification

Selecting a payment method does not confirm that payment has been received. Payments are manually checked before the payment status is marked as Paid.

3. Payment Safety

Customers should only use official payment instructions. Never provide your GCash PIN, bank password, OTP, or other account credentials to anyone.

4. Order Processing

Orders may remain pending while payment or order details are being checked. Service processing begins after the order and any required payment are confirmed.

5. Cancellations

Customers may contact us to request cancellation. Cancellation requests will be reviewed based on the order's current processing stage and applicable laws.

6. Refund Requests

Customers may request a refund for issues such as duplicate payments, overpayments, or services that were paid for but not delivered. Each request will be reviewed fairly and in accordance with applicable consumer protection laws.

7. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, we will contact the customer to arrange the appropriate refund method and explain the expected processing period.
